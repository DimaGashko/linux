# Third-party artwork

`src/openai.svg` and `src/workspaces.svg` contain the OpenAI and `at` icons from
Font Awesome Free 7.3.1, Copyright 2026 Fonticons, Inc., licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Sources:

- https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/blob/7.x/svgs/brands/openai.svg
- https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome/blob/7.x/svgs/solid/at.svg
- https://fontawesome.com/license/free

The SVG paths are scaled uniformly and centered on a 24×24 font canvas; the
outlines are converted to TrueType curves for the generated font. These glyphs
retain the attribution and license above. Other icons and build scripts are MIT.

The OpenAI logo is owned by OpenAI and identifies the OpenAI usage module.
[OpenAI brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/).
